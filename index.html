📍7月6日 北海道美瑛一日游行程表
Biei One-Day Tour Itinerary – July 6

亲爱的各位贵宾，感谢您参加本次北海道美瑛一日游。以下是今天的行程安排，请您参考并留意集合时间。如有变动，导游将另行通知 🙏
Dear guests, thank you for joining our one-day tour to Biei. Below is today’s itinerary. Please be mindful of the meeting times. Any changes will be announced by the guide. 😊

⸻

🕗 08:20–08:30
札幌站集合出发
Depart from Sapporo Station
集合时间为08:10–08:15，请提前到达集合地点以便顺利出发。
Please gather between 08:10–08:15 for a timely departure.

⸻

🕚 11:25–11:55（30 mins）
青池 Blue Pond
欣赏梦幻蓝色池水，适合拍照。
Enjoy the beautiful blue water and take photos.

⸻

🕛 12:00–12:20（20 mins）
白须瀑布 Shirahige Falls
下车步行即达观景桥，景色壮观。
A short walk to the viewing bridge with a scenic waterfall.

⸻

🕧 12:40–14:10（90 mins）
四季彩之丘（午餐 & 自由活动）
Shikisai-no-Oka (Lunch & Free Time)
可自由用餐、拍照、购物。
🍦免费冰淇淋券将在此站发放
Feel free to enjoy lunch, take photos, and shop.
🍦Free ice cream tickets will be handed out at this stop.

⸻

🕝 14:20–14:35（15 mins）
云霄飞车之路（车内观光）
Roller Coaster Road (view from the bus)
乘车经过，可从车窗欣赏起伏美景并拍照。
We’ll drive through this scenic route — enjoy the view and take pictures from your seat.

⸻

🕒 15:00–16:30（90 mins）
富田农场 Farm Tomita
赏花、参观香水馆、品尝薰衣草冰淇淋。
Explore flower gardens, visit the perfume house, and try lavender ice cream.

⸻

🚌 16:30
发车返回札幌（预计车程约2小时）
Depart for Sapporo (approx. 2-hour ride)
请在指定时间准时上车，我们将返回札幌站。
Please be on time for the bus back to Sapporo Station.

⸻
